# Section Blueprints

## Whole-paper throughline

The article follows one contribution-first chain: an established landscape-assisted selection workflow incurs acquisition cost; native search history exists before a separate query; the unresolved decision is whether that query is worth acquiring at the current state; the method defines a matched-state, cost-adjusted target and a pre-query gate; RQ1--RQ5 test utility, predictability beyond time, policy value, transport, and interpretation; Discussion and Conclusion state only what those outputs support. The architecture figure and the RQ map are the two principal navigation devices.
