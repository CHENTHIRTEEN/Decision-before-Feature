## Paper-level learning

**X1** opens from feature-acquisition overhead, introduces trajectory features, and only then specifies the warm-started action-selection task. Its transferable move is this problem-to-design progression; its published performance must not be reused.

**X2** names the default optimizer, switch time, portfolio, information source, warm start, prediction target, and suites early. Its BBOB-to-YABBOB discussion treats transport as a test whose failure is informative, not as an assumed benefit.

**X3** frames independent sampling through budget reduction and missed problem--solver interaction, then defines a longitudinal representation. The transferable move is to place FE, time, and memory accounting beside the query decision while avoiding its algorithm- and dimension-dependent construction.

**X4** defines behavioral categories before detailed measures. This concept-before-metric pattern fits progress, diversity, entropy, stagnation, and distance behavior; its known-optimum and identity-dependent measures remain inadmissible gate inputs.

**X5** separates statistical detectability from practical importance and bounds conclusions to tested optimizers and benchmarks. Its claim discipline transfers; its executive-summary tone does not.

**X6** makes split difficulty and SBS comparison part of the scientific argument. It supports RQ-aligned Results in which held-out BBOB families and each external suite receive separate estimates and conclusions.

## Structural Patterns

1. **Narrow before expanding.** Define task, information time, target, and action before surveying neighboring methods.
2. **Separate representation, prediction, and action.** Keep information source, learning target, transition, and the preceding query-acquisition layer distinct.
3. **Let evaluation design carry the claim.** Follow RQ contrasts and replication units, with limitations beside the affected claim.

## Rhetorical Patterns

- Open from an accepted workflow's limitation, then state the unresolved decision.
- Close Related Work subsections with a contrast in decision, information, label, cost, transition, or split.
- Present external benchmarks as tests of transport, never assumed successes.

## Language Patterns

Use a restrained technical register: “formulates,” “evaluates,” and “is associated with.” Name metric direction, effect, interval, and scope. Prefer *query*, *acquisition cost*, *native continuation*, *population-transfer initialization*, and *best observed action*. Avoid universal novelty, causal language, zero-cost claims, and local execution prose.
